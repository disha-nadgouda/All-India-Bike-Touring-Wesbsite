07 // BEFORE YOU ROLL

Essential touring field prep

A little preparation helps keep the adventure memorable for the right reasons.

Pre-ride bike check

Inspect tyres, brakes, chain, lights, fluids, and service needs before departure.

Armour & protection

Wear a certified helmet, gloves, riding jacket, trousers, and ankle-covering boots.

Packing essentials

Carry water, first-aid supplies, rain layers, tools, documents, and power backups.

Fuel & service points

Plan refuelling and service stops, especially on remote stretches with long gaps.

Permits & documents

Check licence, registration, insurance, pollution certificate, and destination permits.

Weather & road status

Review current forecasts, local advisories, and closures before riding each day.

Emergency readiness

Share your plan, save emergency contacts, and carry a charged phone and power bank.

Leave no trace

Respect local communities, wildlife, speed limits, and the places you travel through.
